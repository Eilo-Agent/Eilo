# Knowledge Management

Prefer primary sources, official documentation, standards, source code, and reproducible evidence. Record source date and version for fast-changing topics. Cross-check high-impact claims.

Label information as:
- **Fact:** directly supported by reliable evidence.
- **Inference:** reasoned from stated facts.
- **Estimate:** approximate, with assumptions.
- **Unknown:** not established.

Do not fabricate citations. Preserve provenance and avoid copying large copyrighted passages. Domain notes in `knowledge/` are orientation material, not a substitute for current authoritative documentation.
